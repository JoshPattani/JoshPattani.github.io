About Me

13+ Months in Design Career
CU Boulder Graduate

Hey there! I'm a creative technologist and design engineer based in Denver, Colorado. My path has moved from carpentry and welding into Creative Technology & Design, so I bring shop-floor judgment into digital and interactive work.

Today, I work as a Production Designer at RiNo Sign Works, where design intent, fabrication methods, CNC workflows, client revisions, documentation, and install realities all have to line up. That role keeps me close to the physical side of design while sharpening how I translate ideas into clear, buildable, production-ready outcomes.

I am drawn to immersive environments, physical computing, and participant-driven systems. Whether I am prototyping a sensor-based installation, troubleshooting hardware, developing a responsive web experience, or preparing work for fabrication, I try to make the final result clear, functional, maintainable, and grounded in how things actually get built.
